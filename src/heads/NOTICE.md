# Head assets

These `*.facerhead.json` files are the base heads FaceGun loads at startup (FaceR bundles none).
They were exported from the generator sources archived in `tools/head-sources/`.

- `european_scan`: derived from the **Lee Perry-Smith "Infinite" head scan**, © Infinite-Realities
  (<https://triplegangers.com>, <http://www.ir-ltd.net>), licensed under
  [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Decimated, warped into FaceR's
  head frame, eye openings cut. Anyone redistributing an app that ships this head must keep this attribution.
- `african_sculpt` and the archetypes derived from it (`boggie_sculpt`, `walt_classic`, `east_asian`, `nordic_angular`,
  `gracile_feminine`): derived from the ZBrush sculpt **`african_head.obj` by Vidar Rapp**, as distributed with the
  [tinyrenderer](https://github.com/ssloy/tinyrenderer) tutorial. Symmetrized across X=0 and re-topologized with
  lip-separation seams; the archetypes are procedural variations.
